## Grammar and gate

Custom grammar: production dossier. A persistent textual index gives direct access to credentials; identity and the first real work object share the opening; a source-frame projector is the central collection; complete career and skills remain plain ledgers; the ending is a contact plate plus the original export. Bans: marketing claims, pinned persuasion, wheel trapping, ornamental 3D, fake software controls, hidden essential biography, card-based CV content.

The eight stock grammars were considered. Filmic one-shot and continuous world prevent fast recruiter access; chaptered editorial forbids the real work encounter above the fold; live surface would imply a software product; typographic poster would suppress portfolio assets; gallery/catalog is nearest but lacks the identity-first credential navigation required by a CV; split stage lacks an honest comparison; rhythmic cutlist hurries substantive experience. This custom grammar changes navigation, sequence, hero, and close together.

Fingerprint: production dossier | textual section index | identity + dimensional real reel frame | identity / quiet biography / interactive collection / career / skills / education / contact | contact plate with original export | source contact-sheet projector.

Registry has no prior rows. Gate passes with no comparisons required.
